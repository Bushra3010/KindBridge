## Who may join

KindBridge is for adults aged 18 and over. Community approval is not a background check, professional certification or guarantee of safety. Do not invite children to create accounts or use another person's account.

## Everyday help only

Offer only help you can safely provide. The pilot prohibits medical, legal, financial, hazardous, regulated, childcare and personal-care services, and private-home visits. Arrange help online or in a mutually agreed shared space. Never ask for an account password, PIN, financial credential or OTP.

## Respect other members

We prohibit harassment, discrimination, hate, threats, stalking, bullying, scams, impersonation, spam, sexual solicitation, explicit sexual content, nonconsensual intimate images, dangerous or illegal activity, and infringements of intellectual property. Do not publish private information about yourself or anyone else in listings or reviews. Do not pressure a member to share contact details, move off-platform or continue an uncomfortable interaction.

## Child safety: zero tolerance

Child sexual abuse and exploitation are prohibited, including child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, sexualization of minors, solicitation, sextortion, trafficking, and attempts to arrange exploitative contact. This applies to text, images, links, profiles, messages and any other supported content. The adults-only audience does not excuse content or conduct involving harm to a child.

Report suspected child exploitation through Report a problem or {{CHILD_SAFETY_EMAIL}}. Identify the account, listing, message or exchange and describe the concern. Do not download, forward or attach illegal images to a report. Where there is immediate danger, contact local emergency services or the relevant child-protection authority directly.

We assess reports, restrict access to suspected prohibited material and take appropriate account action. We preserve only necessary evidence securely and report confirmed CSAM or other matters to the relevant authority or reporting body as required by applicable law. Authorized staff handle these cases under a restricted process; ordinary members should not investigate or confront suspects.

## Honest exchanges and coins

Agree task details before accepting, communicate changes, confirm only work actually received and write fair reviews. Do not fake exchanges, buy or sell help coins, demand cash redemption, trade reviews or abuse grants. Use the dispute controls for incomplete or contested help.

## Meet safely

Choose a common area such as a clubhouse, library or coworking lounge. Tell someone you trust where you are going, make your own travel arrangements and leave if you feel unsafe. Do not share your home address. KindBridge does not provide emergency response or guarantee member conduct.

## Reporting, blocking and appeals

Use Report a problem for a profile, listing, message, review or exchange. Use Block to prevent new proposals and free-form messages from a member; completion, cancellation, dispute and reporting actions on existing exchanges may remain available. Manage blocked members in Privacy & sharing → Blocked accounts.

We aim to triage ordinary reports within one business day during {{SUPPORT_HOURS}}. Safety cases requiring urgent attention use our escalation process. This is not a promise of round-the-clock monitoring. We may remove content, issue restrictions or suspend/close accounts. Ask for a review at {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} and include the case reference; do not include other members' unnecessary private information.
