## How we use information

We use information for the purposes above, to send verification and necessary service communications, respond to support requests, prevent misuse, comply with applicable obligations and manage disputes. Where available, optional opportunity digests are controlled through notification preferences. We will seek any consent required for optional uses before starting them. Withdrawing optional consent does not affect earlier lawful processing; some core processing is necessary to provide an account or exchange.

## Who can see information

Other eligible community members can see information intended for the community, such as your username, avatar, profile, listings and published reviews. Exchange participants can see their session terms and conversation. A private thank-you note is intended for its recipient rather than the general community.

Optional private contact fields are not displayed to other members unless you share a particular field with a particular member through Share details. You can revoke future in-app access in Privacy & sharing. Revocation cannot retrieve copies or screenshots someone already saved. A photo URL may contact its external host when opened or rendered; that host may receive technical request information.

Authorized staff, organizers and moderators receive access appropriate to their duties, such as membership administration, support and safety investigations. Private-by-default member sharing does not mean the operator cannot access information for those purposes. KindBridge does not describe exchange messaging as end-to-end encrypted.

## Cookies and device permissions

The current web application uses `kb_session` to keep you signed in, with a session lifetime of up to 30 days, and `kb_theme` to remember appearance preferences for up to one year. Signing out removes the active session cookie. You can also use browser/device controls to remove stored data, which may sign you out or reset preferences.

The described pilot does not request device GPS, contacts, microphone or camera access. Meeting spots are entered manually, and private photos are entered as links. {{FINAL_NATIVE_PERMISSION_AND_SDK_NOTICE}}.
